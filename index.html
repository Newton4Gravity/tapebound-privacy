Tapebound — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Tapebound is an audiobook player for Android, developed by Adalberto Enamorado ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what data Tapebound does and does not handle.

The short version

Tapebound does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Audiobook files you choose. Tapebound uses Android's system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). You select a folder; the app reads the audio files and cover images inside it so it can build your library and play your books. The app only ever accesses folders you have explicitly granted it, and that access can be revoked at any time in Android settings.
  • Playback state stored on your device. Your library, listening progress, bookmarks, and settings such as playback speed and sleep-timer preferences are stored in an on-device database and preference storage so the app can resume where you left off. This information never leaves your device.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, Tapebound shows playback controls and sleep-timer information while a book is playing. Denying the permission does not stop playback.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking of any kind.
  • No account creation, sign-in, or user profiling.
  • Your audiobook files are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling Tapebound removes its stored library data and preferences. Your audiobook files themselves are yours and are not modified or deleted by uninstalling.

Children

Tapebound is a general-audience media player and does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Tapebound changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at this URL before the change takes effect.
